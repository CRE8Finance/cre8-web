How it’s put together

A single token, TQQQ, that tracks ProShares UltraPro QQQ (3x Nasdaq-100), an ETF that aims for three times the daily move of its index. Leveraged portfolios are kept separate from the theme portfolios.

How the return is calculated

The APY is a model figure, not a track record. CRE8 takes the portfolio’s current target weights and runs them through the daily closing prices of each asset (stocks and ETFs adjusted for dividends and splits, crypto from Binance market data, XAUt from the gold price). The result is compounded and annualized over Nov 21, 2024 to Nov 21, 2026.

An asset with less than 30 days of price history is left out until it has 30 days, and its weight is shared among the others until then.

Past model returns don’t predict future results, and testnet balances move on simulated prices.

Risks

  • Leveraged portfolios are bought in one go, with no gradual buying and no yield strategy.
  • Prices go down as well as up, and you can lose money. This portfolio holds only 1 asset, so it’s concentrated.
  • Stocks are held as bStock tokens, backed by shares held by the issuer, a Binance affiliate, which publishes a daily proof of reserves and can freeze tokens. That isn’t the same as owning the shares at a broker.
  • Stock tokens only trade while the US market is open. Some on-chain markets are thin, so large orders move the price.
