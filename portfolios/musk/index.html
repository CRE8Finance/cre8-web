Where the list comes from

This one isn’t a disclosure portfolio. Elon Musk doesn’t publish a personal stock list, so the basket holds the listed companies he runs, Tesla and SpaceX, plus bitcoin, which both companies hold on their balance sheets, and a small position in Dogecoin.

Tesla, SpaceX and Bitcoin make up the core at about 31.7% each, with 5% in Dogecoin.

How the return is calculated

The APY is a model figure, not a track record. CRE8 takes the portfolio’s current target weights and runs them through the daily closing prices of each asset (stocks and ETFs adjusted for dividends and splits, crypto from Binance market data, XAUt from the gold price). The result is compounded and annualized over Nov 26, 2024 to Nov 26, 2026.

An asset with less than 30 days of price history is left out until it has 30 days, and its weight is shared among the others until then. SpaceX (SPCX) joins the model on Jul 12, 2026.

The portfolio rebalances once a month, and whenever an asset drifts more than 5 points from its target weight. Past model returns don’t predict future results, and testnet balances move on simulated prices.

Risks

  • Prices go down as well as up, and you can lose money. This portfolio holds only 4 assets, so it’s concentrated, and the crypto in it can swing far more than stocks.
  • Stocks are held as bStock tokens, issued by BTech Holdings, a Binance affiliate. Each token is backed 1:1 by shares held with a custodian, and the issuer publishes a daily proof of collateral. Binance states that bStocks are not shares, so this isn’t the same as owning the shares at a broker.
  • Stock tokens only trade while the US market is open. Some on-chain markets are thin, so large orders move the price.
