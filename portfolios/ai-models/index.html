How it’s put together

CRE8 picks the companies that fit the theme and can be bought on-chain with enough liquidity. Names with deep markets form the core; names with thinner markets get about 5% each.

Anthropic, Amazon, Alphabet, Microsoft, SpaceX and Meta get about 16.7% each.

How the return is calculated

The APY is a model figure, not a track record. CRE8 takes the portfolio’s current target weights and runs them through the daily closing prices of each asset (stocks and ETFs adjusted for dividends and splits, crypto from Binance market data, XAUt from the gold price). The result is compounded and annualized over Nov 22, 2024 to Nov 22, 2026.

An asset with less than 30 days of price history is left out until it has 30 days, and its weight is shared among the others until then. SpaceX (SPCX) joins the model on Jul 12, 2026. Anthropic (ANT) has less than 30 days of price history and isn’t in the model yet.

The portfolio rebalances once a month, and whenever an asset drifts more than 5 points from its target weight. Past model returns don’t predict future results, and testnet balances move on simulated prices.

Risks

  • Prices go down as well as up, and you can lose money. This portfolio holds only 6 assets, so it’s concentrated.
  • Stocks are held as bStock tokens, backed by shares held by the issuer, a Binance affiliate, which publishes a daily proof of reserves and can freeze tokens. That isn’t the same as owning the shares at a broker.
  • Stock tokens only trade while the US market is open. Some on-chain markets are thin, so large orders move the price.
