Where the list comes from

Nancy Pelosi’s household reports its stock trades to the US House of Representatives in Periodic Transaction Reports, usually 30 to 45 days after the trade. This portfolio is built from those filings.

Many of the trades are call options. The portfolio holds the underlying shares instead. The filings give dollar ranges, not exact amounts, so CRE8 sets the weights: NVIDIA, Alphabet, Microsoft, Amazon and Intel make up the core at about 19% each, with 5% in Broadcom.

How the return is calculated

The APY is a model figure, not a track record. CRE8 takes the portfolio’s current target weights and runs them through the daily closing prices of each asset (stocks and ETFs adjusted for dividends and splits, crypto from Binance market data, XAUt from the gold price). The result is compounded and annualized over Nov 24, 2024 to Nov 24, 2026.

An asset with less than 30 days of price history is left out until it has 30 days, and its weight is shared among the others until then.

The portfolio rebalances once a month, and whenever an asset drifts more than 5 points from its target weight. Past model returns don’t predict future results, and testnet balances move on simulated prices.

Risks

  • Prices go down as well as up, and you can lose money. This portfolio holds only 6 assets, so it’s concentrated.
  • Filings are delayed, so the portfolio reflects what has been disclosed, not what is held today.
  • Stocks are held as bStock tokens, issued by BTech Holdings, a Binance affiliate. Each token is backed 1:1 by shares held with a custodian, and the issuer publishes a daily proof of collateral. Binance states that bStocks are not shares, so this isn’t the same as owning the shares at a broker.
  • Stock tokens only trade while the US market is open. Some on-chain markets are thin, so large orders move the price.
