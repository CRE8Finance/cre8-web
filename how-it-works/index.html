How it works

CRE8 lets you buy a basket of US stock tokens and crypto with USDT, in one payment, and keep it in an on-chain account that belongs to you. Cash you haven’t invested can earn in the meantime.

Buying

  1. Sign in with email (or Google). CRE8 opens a smart account for you on BNB Chain, owned by the wallet you signed in with.
  2. Add USDT. On testnet you can claim free test USDT in the app.
  3. Pick a portfolio and enter an amount, 50 USDT minimum. One payment buys every asset in the basket at its target weight.

Trades go through an allowlist of on-chain venues (PancakeSwap, KyberSwap and OpenOcean). Each asset has a per-trade cap, so large orders are split into several trades.

After that the portfolio keeps its shape: it rebalances once a month, and whenever an asset drifts more than 5 points from its target weight. You can also build your own basket of stocks and weights, and CRE8 rebalances that too.

Who holds the assets

You do. Every user has their own smart account on BNB Chain, and the tokens sit there, never pooled with anyone else’s, where any block explorer can show them. CRE8’s automation can only run trades inside your account. Moving anything out of it takes your signature.

US stocks are held as bStock, tokenized US stocks on BNB Chain issued by BTech Holdings, a Binance affiliate. Each bStock is backed 1:1 by shares held with a custodian, and the issuer publishes a daily proof of collateral. Holding bStock isn’t the same as owning shares at a broker: Binance states that bStocks are not shares, and dividends are paid as extra tokens after a 30% US withholding tax rather than as cash.

Fees

  • Testnet: no fees, and CRE8 pays the gas.
  • Mainnet: fees will be announced before launch. Venue pool fees and slippage apply on top.

Selling and withdrawing

Sell some or all of a portfolio back to USDT and the money lands in your account. From there you can withdraw USDT to any BNB Chain address, including an exchange deposit address. Large sales go through in a few trades because of the per-asset cap.

When the US market is closed

Stock tokens only trade during US market hours, and CRE8 doesn’t force trades outside them. An order placed while the market is closed waits in your account and goes through at the next open. Until then your USDT stays in your own account.

Earning on idle USDT

Cash in your account can just sit there, or you can press Earn to put it in one of CRE8’s allowlisted USDT vaults (Lista, Venus, Aave). Each vault shows its APY, which follows market rates; CRE8 doesn’t promise a rate. Withdraw any amount, interest included, whenever you like. CRE8 never moves your cash unless you ask it to.

Yield strategy on a portfolio

When you buy a portfolio you can also turn on the yield strategy. It stays off unless you switch it on. CRE8 then uses the portfolio’s shares to earn a bit more: it deposits them in a lending market, takes out a small, conservative amount of USDT against them and puts that in a USDT vault. If prices fall to the safety line, the system automatically repays part of the loan from the vault, without selling your shares, and your activity log tells you afterwards how much was repaid. You can switch it off at any time and the shares stay in the portfolio.

CRE8 takes out only a small part of what the market would allow, which keeps the risk low but can’t remove it entirely. Leveraged portfolios don’t offer the yield strategy.

Your cash and each portfolio’s money are always kept apart. USDT a portfolio uses to earn extra belongs to that portfolio only: it never counts as your cash and never mixes with another portfolio.

On testnet the vaults and rates are simulated, so treat the numbers as illustrations.

It’s a testnet

  • CRE8 runs on BNB Chain testnet with test versions of the tokens and simulated prices. No real money is involved.
  • Test USDT is free. Signing in needs an invite code.
  • There’s no mainnet date yet. The contracts get an external audit before mainnet.
  • Anyone outside the US can use it, where local rules allow.
