Questions

Is this real money?+

Not yet. CRE8 is on BNB Chain testnet, and test USDT is free. There’s no mainnet date yet.

Is CRE8 safe?+

Nothing on CRE8 involves real money yet. Your portfolio sits in your own smart account on BNB Chain. CRE8’s automation can only trade inside it, and moving money out takes your signature. The contracts get an external audit before mainnet.

Who holds my shares?+

You do. They sit as tokens in an on-chain account only you control, never pooled with anyone else’s.

How do I cash out?+

Sell some or all of a portfolio back to USDT, then withdraw the USDT to any BNB Chain address. Stock tokens sell while the US market is open.

How does idle USDT earn?+

Press Earn to put idle USDT in an allowlisted USDT vault (Lista, Venus or Aave) at the market rate, and withdraw it whenever you like. When you buy a portfolio you can also turn on extra yield, which uses its shares to earn a bit more and automatically sells a little if prices fall fast. Your cash and your portfolios are always kept separate.

How is this different from buying stock tokens on an exchange?+

On an exchange you buy one stock at a time and it sits in your exchange account. CRE8 buys a whole basket in one payment, rebalances it to its target weights, keeps it in an account you own on-chain, and lets idle USDT earn. See the comparison.

What happens when the US market is closed?+

Stock tokens only trade during US market hours. A buy or sell placed while the market is closed waits in your account and goes through at the next open.

What’s the minimum?+

50 USDT per purchase.

What does it cost?+

Nothing on testnet, and CRE8 pays the gas. Mainnet fees will be announced before launch.

Who can use it?+

Anyone outside the US, where local rules allow. During testnet you need an invite code to sign in.
