# How to Buy a Basket of US Stocks With USDT: Mag7 and AI Chips

By CRE8 · November 23, 2026 · 7 min read

You can buy a basket of US stocks with USDT by buying each stock token on an exchange, buying an ETF through a broker or crypto platform, or using CRE8 to buy a whole weighted basket in one USDT payment. CRE8's Mag7 portfolio holds the seven stocks in equal weights of about 14.3% each; its AI Chips portfolio holds NVIDIA, TSMC, Intel and SK hynix at 21.25% each plus Broadcom, Marvell and Penguin Solutions at 5% each. After you buy, the weights maintain themselves: curated portfolios rebalance monthly, and whenever any holding drifts more than 5 percentage points from target.

## What do "basket" and "rebalancing" mean?

A basket is a set of stocks held together at fixed target weights. Put 1,000 USDT into the Magnificent Seven and each stock gets about 143 USDT, instead of all of it going into one name.

Rebalancing means pulling the weights back to target. Stocks move at different speeds, so after a while the winners take up more of the basket and the laggards less. A rebalance trims the winners and tops up the laggards until each is back at its target.

CRE8 is an app on BNB Chain for buying baskets of tokenized US stocks and crypto with USDT, held in your own smart account (an on-chain account that belongs to you; moving assets out takes your signature). Stocks are held as bStock, tokenized US stocks issued by BTech Holdings, a Binance affiliate, each backed 1:1 by shares held with a custodian. Binance states that "bStocks are not shares" ([Binance Learn](https://www.binance.com/en/learn/bstocks)).

## How is the Mag7 portfolio weighted?

CRE8's [Mag7 portfolio](https://cre8.finance/portfolios/mag7) is equal-weighted: Apple, Alphabet, Amazon and Meta at 14.29% each, and Microsoft, NVIDIA and Tesla at 14.28% each ([CRE8 llms.txt](https://cre8.finance/llms.txt), checked November 23, 2026).

Equal weight means each company gets the same dollar amount, regardless of market cap. The largest one or two companies don't end up dominating the basket.

The model APY is +13.1% as of November 22, 2026. That's a backtest: current weights applied to daily closing prices from November 22, 2024 to November 22, 2026, compounded and annualized. It is not a track record. The same page shows −13.0% over the last 30 days and +0.4% over one year ([Mag7 portfolio page](https://cre8.finance/portfolios/mag7)).

## How do Mag7 and AI Chips differ?

Both hold NVIDIA, but they're built differently. Mag7 holds the largest US companies, spread across phones, cloud, ads, e-commerce and EVs. AI Chips sits on one supply chain: chip designers like NVIDIA and Broadcom, the foundry TSMC, and memory maker SK hynix.

That shows up in the swings. As of November 22, 2026, Mag7 is −9.0% over 90 days and AI Chips is −31.5%, yet over the two-year backtest AI Chips is far ahead. Mag7 behaves more like a basket of big tech; AI Chips is a single-industry bet that suits people who can stomach large drawdowns.

You can hold both. Each portfolio is separate, rebalances on its own, and its money never mixes with the other.

## How often does rebalancing happen?

Curated portfolios rebalance once a month. A rebalance also triggers right away whenever any holding drifts more than 5 percentage points from its target weight ([How CRE8 works](https://cre8.finance/how-it-works)).

The 5 points are absolute. NVIDIA at a 21.25% target triggers a rebalance above 26.25% or below 16.25%.

Baskets you build yourself get rebalanced too, on a schedule you choose: daily, weekly, monthly or quarterly.

Rebalancing trades go through the same allowlisted venues (PancakeSwap, KyberSwap and OpenOcean), and stock tokens only trade during US market hours. A rebalance that falls due while the market is closed waits for the next open.

## Can you build your own basket?

Yes. Pick your own stocks and weights in CRE8, and it rebalances the basket on the schedule you choose: daily, weekly, monthly or quarterly.

Say you like the Mag7 list but want more NVIDIA and less Tesla. Build it yourself. The minimum is still 50 USDT per purchase.

## What does it look like with 1,000 USDT?

Say you have 1,000 USDT and want the AI Chips portfolio.

In CRE8, open AI Chips, enter 1,000 USDT and confirm once. NVIDIA, TSMC, Intel and SK hynix get 212.50 USDT each; Broadcom, Marvell and Penguin Solutions get 50 USDT each. Large orders are split automatically to stay under each asset's per-trade cap.

Building the same basket on an exchange means seven orders, each sized by hand. Binance's minimum per bStock is $5 ([Binance Learn](https://www.binance.com/en/learn/bstocks)). When the weights drift, you do the math and the trades yourself.

Now the rebalance trigger, using a hypothetical move and ignoring fees and slippage.

If the other six stay flat and NVIDIA rises 30%, it grows from 212.50 to about 276.25 USDT. The basket is about 1,063.75 USDT, so NVIDIA is 25.97% of it. That's 4.72 points over target, under the 5-point trigger, so it waits for the monthly rebalance.

If NVIDIA rises 35% instead, it's about 286.88 USDT in a 1,074.38 USDT basket, or 26.70%. That's 5.45 points over, so a rebalance fires. The system sells about 58.57 USDT of NVIDIA and spreads it across the other six, bringing NVIDIA back to about 228.30 USDT, or 21.25%.

CRE8 is on BNB Chain testnet only. It uses free test USDT and simulated prices, and no real money is involved.

## When is another option the better choice?

You want one ETF and can open a brokerage account: buy the ETF there and you hold real units. Binance Stocks also offers 7,000+ US stocks and ETFs, converting your USDT to USDC when you order, though the holdings are held by its partner broker, Alpaca, and can't be withdrawn to a wallet ([Binance Learn](https://www.binance.com/en/learn/binance-stocks)).

You only want one or two stocks: a single order on Binance is quicker, and you don't need a basket.

You want to trade 24/7: Binance spot trades around the clock, while CRE8 only trades during US market hours.

If you're in mainland China, note that since May 22, 2026, offshore brokers can't accept new purchases or deposits from existing mainland clients ([CSRC](https://www.csrc.gov.cn/csrc/c100028/c7634326/content.shtml)).

## What are the risks?

Prices fall. A basket spreads single-stock risk, not market risk. The Mag7 are all large tech companies and AI Chips sits in one industry, so a sector selloff hits the whole basket.

Model APY is not a promise. It's a backtest of current weights on historical prices; a different window gives a very different number, and testnet balances move on simulated prices.

bStock isn't a share. You hold a claim from the issuer, and dividends arrive as extra tokens after a 30% US withholding tax, not as cash ([Binance Learn](https://www.binance.com/en/learn/bstocks)).

Thin markets cost more. On a shallow on-chain pool, large orders see slippage, meaning a worse fill than the quoted price.

Rebalancing doesn't guarantee gains. It restores the weights, and the stock you trimmed may keep rising.

Smart contract risk. CRE8 runs on smart contracts, which get an external audit before mainnet.

## FAQ

### What's the minimum?

50 USDT per purchase. In AI Chips, that puts 2.50 USDT into each 5% holding.

### Will the holdings change?

They can. The portfolio pages are the source of truth; this article uses the numbers as of November 23, 2026.

### Can I buy when the US market is closed?

You can place the order, but stock tokens only trade during US market hours. The order waits for the next open, and your USDT stays in your own account until then.

### Are there fees?

None on testnet, and CRE8 pays the gas. Mainnet fees will be announced before launch.

### Where does the money go when I sell?

Sell some or all of a portfolio back to USDT; it lands in your account, and you can withdraw it to any BNB Chain address.
