Buying stock tokens on an exchange

Suits people who want one or two popular stocks, or who move large amounts.

If you want a whole theme in one payment, kept on target and held in your own on-chain account, CRE8 is less work.

Buying stock tokens yourself

Suits people who are at home with wallets and check contract addresses themselves.

CRE8 picks the assets, buys them in one payment, and lets idle USDT earn.

Opening a US brokerage account

Suits people whose money is already in a bank and who can open an account easily where they live.

If your money is in USDT and you’d rather not cash out, CRE8 skips the cash-out and account opening.
